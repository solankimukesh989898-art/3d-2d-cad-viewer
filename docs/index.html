What it does

Open a drawing or model, check it, change it and export it again, without a computer.

View 3D

Hardware-accelerated OpenGL ES rendering, wireframe mode, cross-sections, measurement and a view cube.

View 2D

Pan and zoom drawings, inspect layers, and measure slots, arc slots, rectangles and other shapes by their sizes.

Edit

Move, rotate and scale parts, insert primitives, cut along a plane with automatic capping, sculpt meshes, and sketch in 2D with undo and redo.

Nest for cutting

Lay out parts on sheets for laser or plasma cutting, with gaps, margins, remnant lines and a parts list. Optimize finds a tighter layout.

Convert

Save 3D models as STL, OBJ, PLY or GLB, and 2D drawings as DXF or SVG.

Open from anywhere

Open files from your file manager, email or cloud apps with "Open with" or Share. No broad storage access is needed.

Privacy

Your CAD files stay on your device. Precision CAD does not upload drawings, models, project files or conversion results to any server.

The Home screen shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Ads are not shown in the viewers, editors or exports. Before ads load, the app asks for your advertising choices through Google's consent form where required.
