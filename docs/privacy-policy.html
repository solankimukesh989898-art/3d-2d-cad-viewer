Privacy policy

Precision CAD

Last updated 5 November 2026. This policy applies to the version that introduces Google AdMob advertising.

Earlier versions without advertising may have different permissions.

Overview

Precision CAD is an Android CAD viewer, editor and converter. Your CAD documents remain on your device. The app uses Google AdMob to show a banner on the Home screen; the advertising SDK needs internet connectivity.

CAD files and your work

  • Files you explicitly choose or share with the app are opened and processed locally for viewing, editing and conversion.
  • We do not upload CAD document contents, drawings, project files or conversion results to an advertising server.
  • Recent files, thumbnails and settings are held locally on your device.
  • No Precision CAD account is required.

Advertising and data processing

Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process information such as advertising identifiers (where available), IP address, device and app information, interactions with ads, diagnostics and approximate location inferred from network signals. Google may use this information to deliver, measure, secure and, where allowed, personalize advertisements. The exact handling depends on your region, device settings and consent choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and controls

When required, the app asks for your advertising privacy choices through Google's consent form. Advertising requests are held until the UMP SDK indicates ads may be requested. You can also manage ad personalization through Android and Google privacy settings. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads.

Permissions

The app declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for advertising. It does not request broad storage permissions: your documents are accessed through Android's file picker or another app's Share or Open with action.

Security and children

We do not intentionally transmit CAD contents through AdMob. Google processes its advertising data under its own safeguards and policies. Precision CAD is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

Material changes to collection, advertising or SDK use will be reflected in this policy and in the Play Store Data safety disclosure.
